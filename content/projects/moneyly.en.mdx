## PROBLEM

Personal finance scattered across spreadsheets: no consolidated view, no budget alerts and no incentive to stick to savings goals.

## ARCHITECTURE DECISIONS

Front in Next.js and API in Express with Drizzle over PostgreSQL, in separate repos. The contract lives in Zod schemas: they generate the OpenAPI spec and, from it, kubb and orval generate the typed hooks the front consumes — change the schema and the front breaks at compile-time, not in production. TanStack Query handles cache and invalidation.

<Snippet file="db/schema.ts" lang="ts">
{`export const transactions = pgTable('transactions', {
  id: uuid('id').primaryKey().defaultRandom(),
  amount: numeric('amount').notNull(),
  categoryId: uuid('category_id').references(() => categories.id),
  occurredAt: timestamp('occurred_at').notNull(),
});`}
</Snippet>

## RESULT

Full app in production: monthly dashboard, transactions tracked by category, budgets with progress and savings goals.
