## PROBLEM

Outsourcing company with no professional digital presence — the services pitch depended on a PDF sent by email.

## DECISIONS

Static landing in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — zero framework, zero build: loads instantly on any connection. Clear hierarchy: value proposition above the fold, scannable services and a single contact CTA repeated at the decision points.

## RESULT

Page in production serving as the company's commercial business card.
